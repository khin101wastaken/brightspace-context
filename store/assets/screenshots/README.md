Each screenshot is labelled “Synthetic demonstration data.” No real catalog, documents, token or student information is included. The synthetic preview is development-only and is excluded from the upload ZIP.

- `02-search.jpg`: dark theme, an unfinished-word search, module-note and filename highlighting.
- `03-sync.jpg`: two completed segments out of five; the currently scanning course remains neutral.

All three are full-bleed 1280×800 RGB JPEGs. They illustrate the interface, not a claim that a reviewer has NYU access. The other sample course outlines intentionally show unavailable until synced.
